How it works

From "tell us about your problem" to a ready-to-call enquiry

1

Clara asks

What happened, who the other side is, what's at stake, key dates, documents, and what the client wants. One question at a time, in plain English.

2

Clara flags

Court papers, hearing dates, deadlines and possession issues are marked urgent, and the client is told to call you today.

3

You receive

A structured summary by email with the area of law, a lead score, the other side's name for a conflict check and the best time to call.

Built for law firms

Careful by design

Clara gathers facts and signposts. Advice, judgement and the client relationship stay with your solicitors.

No legal advice, ever

Clara never says whether someone has a case, predicts outcomes or quotes fees. If asked, it explains a solicitor will advise on the call.

Urgent matters flagged

Anything with a court date, deadline or time limit is marked urgent at the top of the email, so it isn't missed over a weekend.

Conflict check details

Every enquiry includes the other side's name, so you can check for conflicts before you call.

Your wording, approved by you

Clara's greeting, questions and disclaimers are agreed with your firm before it goes live.

Clear that it's an AI

Clara always says it's an AI assistant for the firm, never a solicitor or a member of staff.

Data protection in place

Your firm is the controller and Clara is your processor, with a data processing agreement and a short privacy notice for your site.
