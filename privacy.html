This notice explains how Clara Disputes uses personal information on this website, including the live demo.

Who we are

Clara Disputes is run by Colin Williams, a sole trader based in Wolverhampton, England. Colin is the data controller for this website and is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. Contact: colin@colinwilliams.space or 07724 335098.

The demo

The demo is for law firms to see how Clara works. Brindley Vale Solicitors is a fictional firm and nobody will contact you about anything you type. Please use made-up names and details.

  • What you type is sent to Anthropic, the company that provides the AI model, so Clara can reply. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models.
  • We don't save demo conversations in a database. The summary at the end is shown only in your browser and is not emailed to anyone.
  • Our host, Vercel, keeps short-lived technical logs, such as your network address, to keep the service running and stop misuse.

If you contact us

If you call or email us about Clara, we use your details only to reply and arrange a demo, and keep them for up to two years unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

When a law firm uses Clara

When Clara runs on a law firm's own website, that firm is the data controller for its enquiries and Clara Disputes acts as its processor under a written data processing agreement. The firm's own privacy notice explains how it uses enquiry information.

Our lawful basis

We rely on legitimate interests: running and protecting this website and responding to enquiries from businesses.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will reply within one month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
