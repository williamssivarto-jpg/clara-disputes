What Clara is

Clara is a software tool for law firms. It gathers information from people who contact a firm and passes it to the firm in a structured summary. Clara does not give legal advice, and Clara Disputes is not a law firm and is not regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority.

The demo

The demo on this site uses a fictional firm, Brindley Vale Solicitors. It is for demonstration only. Nothing Clara says in the demo is legal advice, and nobody will act on what you type. Please use made-up details. Don't use the demo for real legal problems; if you need help, contact a solicitor or Citizens Advice.

Fair use

Please don't try to misuse the demo, overload it, or use it for anything other than seeing how Clara works. We may limit or withdraw access to keep the service running.

Services for law firms
